## Hard gates (any single failure = reject, score ≤ 4)
1. Any lip-synced AI dialogue, or on-camera talking characters. (An off-screen narrator voiceover is wanted since 2026-10-08, but only a voice the client picked by ear from candidates.)
2. A slapstick, costume or gag premise, or a "sketch comedy" tone.
3. The viewer can't say what Optinet offers by 3 seconds with the sound off.
4. The message is about a customer's trade rather than Optinet's offer.
5. No premium motion graphics (glass cards, kinetic type, branded end card).
6. An AI-sung brand line, or a narrator voice the client has not picked by ear. A narrator with any robotic tell (flat prosody, wrong stress, clicks) fails.
8. No narrator voiceover in a master ad (client verdict 2026-10-08: music-only feels unfinished).
7. AI tells in hero shots: crowds of faces, typing hands in close-up, readable screens, morphing props, warped hands.

## Scored criteria (1–10 each)
- **Entertaining:** is there a moment (surprise, escalation, visual payoff) a viewer would retell? Below 6 = reject, however clean the ad is.
- **Promotional:** is a concrete offer or proof shown in the scene (what you get, a real number, the result on screen, the brand said 3+ times, a call to action before the end card)? Below 6 = reject.
- **Premium feel:** would it sit next to an Apple, Stripe or Notion brand film?
- **Clarity:** "your whole digital team on demand: SEO, AI automation, design, support", understood muted.
- **Ownable idea:** one visual device that is Optinet's and repeats in every cut-down.
- **Craft fit for AI tools:** shots AI video does well (empty, beautifully lit spaces; objects; light; slow camera moves) rather than shots it does badly (acting, talking faces, hands, crowds, fast action).
- **Template value:** can the same structure be reused for any other business with new inputs?
- **Predicted client reaction:** "yes", "maybe" or "no", with one sentence on why.
